What’s new

The short version. The full changelog is in the app (Options → Changelog).

0.4.16

  • Calmer rose animation: petals no longer swing in from far away.
  • The music video button sits on the lyrics screen only.
  • In a Rose, turning on video mode no longer pops the lyrics screen open for everyone.

0.4.15

  • Rose sessions share music video mode and the clip, with one clock for everyone; a crossfade can't undo a skip any more.
  • An update arrow in the top bar, a full-window video mode, and smoother lyrics and startup animations.

0.4.14

  • Share links open in a small window with the song, the link, a Copy button and when it expires.
  • Menus open their submenus as you hover.
  • Song mode always plays the song, never the music video’s audio; pick the clip right from video mode.
  • Follow artists: their new releases and songs come up first, and they get a row on Home.
  • Your stats look like a profile: totals, your current obsession and recent tracks.
  • “Set” only shows when other versions really exist; Liked Songs in the mini player; big playlists open faster.

0.4.13

  • A clearer song menu, choose the music video yourself, your own name and picture for any song.
  • Last.fm scrobbling, and a background in the cover’s own colours.

0.4.12

  • Your own audio files, sharing with Petals and by link, Spotify import, sorting and a back button.

0.4.7 – 0.4.11

  • Clips only from official channels, lyrics that follow the video, a cleaner video screen and many fixes.

0.3 – 0.4.6

  • Petals (friends), Roses (listening together), your stats, Home discovery, Talk-through, Czech and Russian.

0.1 – 0.2

  • The first Velvet: YouTube Music songs and videos, synced lyrics, playlists, smart shuffle and the mini player.

Privacy

Your library lives on your PC. An account is optional: with one, your library syncs to Velvet’s server, and your Petals see what you listen to only if you allow it. Velvet doesn’t sell or share your data, and has no ads or trackers.

This website keeps no cookies. It asks GitHub for the latest version. Feedback sends only what you write; Cloudflare checks that you aren’t a bot, and your IP address is kept only as a scrambled code, for 90 days, to stop spam.
